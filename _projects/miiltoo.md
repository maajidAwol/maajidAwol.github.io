**Role:** Team lead, backend developer (team of 5) · **When:** Aug–Nov 2023

**Tech:** Python, Flask, MySQL, OpenAI API

### Context

In 2022/2023 only 3.2% of Ethiopian students scored above the 50% passing point on the Grade 12 national exam. Miiltoo is a generative-AI study companion built to help students prepare, combining a chatbot for questions with a quiz bot for practice.

### What I did

- Led a team of five through design and development.
- Built the backend with Flask, MySQL, and the OpenAI API, handling over 1,000 daily user requests with response times under 200 ms.

### Recognition

Ranked in the **top 9** at the **A2SV Generative AI for Africa Hackathon**, out of 3,700+ participants from 47 countries.
